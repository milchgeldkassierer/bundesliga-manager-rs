# DOSBox side of the crest editor scenario (tools/dosbox-capture.sh syntax, seconds).
# exe: TEAMED.EXE
# DOSBox's pointer lands up to two pixels off the requested x in the 242-line mode; the port
# script uses the x positions DOSBox reaches.
# In the scrolled view of the import (mouse y range 87..328) DOSBox maps the window's 242
# rows onto y 0..328 (about y * 329 / 242) and clamps to 87: the y values there are chosen
# to land on the port's driver positions (99 -> 135, 94 -> 128, 104 -> 142, 100 -> 136,
# 60 -> 87).
wait 5
# Select club 0 (1.FC Kaiserslautern): the crest editor.
click 21 100 242
wait 2
shot c01-editor
# Right button in the enlarged crest: colour pick with a frame around the field.
click 61 130 242 3
wait 1
shot c02-pick-colour
# A colour field (row 2, column 7: yellow).
click 309 128 242
wait 1
shot c03-colour-field
# Three strokes of one pixel each.
click 41 120 242
wait 1
click 45 124 242
wait 1
click 81 160 242
wait 1
shot c04-painted
# Undo takes back the last stroke only.
click 201 176 242
wait 2
shot c05-undo
# Ur-Wappen restores the crest picture.
click 201 192 242
wait 2
shot c06-original
# Paint again and AENDERUNG SICHERN: PIC\88.VGA is written, the grid shows the new crest.
click 41 120 242
wait 1
click 78 65 242
wait 2
shot c07-saved
# Club 1, Iff-Bild laden: the scrolled view with the browser (C:\).
click 60 100 242
wait 2
click 201 160 242
wait 2
shot c08-browser
# Into the directory IFF (row 3). The DOSBox mouse maps the window to y 0..241 and clamps it to 87..328, so positions are driver positions: the pointer is drawn 87 rows higher.
click 33 99 242
wait 2

# CREST.IFF (row 2): first click picks the name, the second loads it.
click 33 94 242

click 101 100 242

click 201 160 242
wait 2
